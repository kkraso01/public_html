About Me

Profile

I design end-to-end AI systems that bridge research and production constraints. My work centers on LLM orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multi-model routing — systems that balance latency, cost, and accuracy at scale. I approach AI with a reliability mindset: monitoring, evaluation, failure modes, and deployment tradeoffs are first-class concerns.

Professionally, I bring software engineering discipline (QA automation, DevOps, backend APIs) and research infrastructure experience (deploying local LLMs, ChromaDB integration, Docker orchestration). I build and test systems under constraints: latency, cost, reliability, and observability. The robotics and autonomy projects on this site are simulation-first hobby work — not formal coursework — demonstrating my interest in perception-to-control loops, planning, and physics-based systems.

I build AI as systems, not just models.
