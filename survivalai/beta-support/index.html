Beta Support

This page is for SurvivalAI beta users. Clear reports help isolate issues quickly and improve release quality.

How to Report a Bug

Send one report per issue and keep the title specific. If possible, reproduce the problem at least once before reporting.

  • Use a short title that describes the failure.
  • Describe what you expected to happen.
  • Describe what happened instead.
  • Include whether the issue is always reproducible or intermittent.

What Details to Include

  • SurvivalAI app version (for example, 0.9.3-beta).
  • Android version (for example, Android 14).
  • Device model and chipset where possible.
  • Exact reproduction steps in order.
  • Screenshots or short screen recordings.
  • Relevant logs or error messages, if available.
  • Whether network, GPS, microphone, or file access was enabled at the time.

Known Limitations

  • Beta builds may have reduced stability under high load or low-memory conditions.
  • Offline maps and downloaded assets can consume significant device storage.
  • Behavior may vary across vendors due to background process and battery policies.
  • Some features may require network connectivity unless fully cached beforehand.

Response Expectations

Reports are reviewed in priority order based on severity and reproducibility. Critical crashes and data-loss issues are addressed first.

  • Initial acknowledgement target: within 3 business days.
  • Follow-up timing depends on complexity and available diagnostics.
  • Fixes are typically delivered in the next suitable beta iteration.
