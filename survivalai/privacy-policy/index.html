Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how SurvivalAI handles information when you use the Android app.

Last updated: 19 March 2026

Information We Collect

SurvivalAI is designed to process most operational data on your device. Depending on features you use, the app may process location signals, voice input, map context, cached files, and usage events.

  • On-device inputs such as text prompts, route preferences, and selected app settings.
  • Optional sensor-derived context such as approximate or precise location.
  • Optional audio data when microphone-based features are used.
  • Local file references when you choose to import or export supported files.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate core features, improve reliability, and provide user-requested functionality.

  • Generate in-app responses and assistive outputs.
  • Support offline navigation logic and local decision tools.
  • Store preferences and maintain app state between sessions.
  • Diagnose failures when you explicitly share diagnostics.

Permissions

SurvivalAI may request permissions only for features you choose to use.

  • Location: for map positioning, route context, and location-aware suggestions.
  • Microphone: for optional voice commands or audio capture features.
  • Storage or Files: to read, cache, and manage map or model assets, or files you select.
  • Network Access: to download updates, map packs, model assets, or optional online services.

Data Storage

SurvivalAI supports offline behavior where practical. Offline map data, downloaded model assets, and cache files may be stored on your device to improve speed and availability.

By default, data remains local. Information is not shared externally unless you explicitly enable a feature that requires external processing or intentionally submit data (for example, sending feedback or diagnostics).

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party Android system components or optional service providers for analytics, crash reporting, network delivery, or map/model distribution. These services are used only when enabled and are subject to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

SurvivalAI is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the site owner to request review and deletion where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as app features evolve. Material updates will be reflected by changing the last updated date on this page.
