Informations and Services

Opening hours

Late arrival and automatic arrival are available.

  • Monday to Thursday: 6:30 am to 9:00 pm.
  • Friday and Saturday: 24h/24h.
  • Sunday and holidays: 7:30 am to 12:00 pm and 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm.

The automatic credit card reception allows you to take a room 24h/24.

Information

  • Check-in from 2:00 pm and check-out by 11:30 am.
  • Late arrival is possible through the automatic arrival facility.
  • Holiday checks accepted.
  • Pets accepted, subject to applicable conditions and supplement.
  • Free parking.
  • Train station to Saint Omer (5 km).

Services

  • Free Wifi available in the rooms.
  • Vending machines for drinks, confectionery and snacks.
  • Microwave available during opening hours.
  • Hair dryer, iron and ironing board available free of charge.
  • Cots, high chair and changing table available free of charge.
  • Fax and photocopies.
