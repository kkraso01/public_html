Haven — Free Boutique Hotel & Resort Template (uiCookies)

Concept: A single-page site for a small, character-rich coastal resort — the opposite of a corporate chain theme. The layout idea is a full-bleed hero whose "Book a Stay" date widget floats down and overlaps the section below it, setting an unhurried, editorial rhythm that carries through an asymmetric welcome block, a per-night rooms grid, a masonry gallery with lightbox, a dark dining feature, and a review carousel. Palette (warm, luxe-calm, deliberately not the usual teal-on-white or cream-and-terracotta hotel cliché): warm sand ground #EFE6D6 with softer #F8F2E8 panels, deep aubergine #45303F for the dark bands and footer, warm cocoa ink #2A2420 for text, honey-amber #BD7A38 as the single accent, and muted taupe #877868 for secondary copy. Type pairing: Fraunces (an optical, softly wonky display serif) for headings and prices, set against Jost (a clean geometric sans) for body and UI — characterful but calm. Built on self-hosted Bootstrap 5 with vanilla JS (no jQuery) and inline-SVG icons only, so it deploys clean on strict-CSP hosts.
